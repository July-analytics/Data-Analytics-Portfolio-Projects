## Superstore Sales & Customer Analytics
## Project Objective
Analyze sales trends, product performance, and customer segments across a 3-page interactive Power BI report. 
 ## Tools & Concepts Used
-	Tools: Power BI, Power Query, DAX
-	Data Model: Star Schema (Fact_Sales + 4 Dimensions) 
-	Cleaning: Table merging, null imputation (00000), date delimiter parsing
-	DAX: SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR, DIVIDE, CALCULATE, DISTINCTCOUNT, COUNTROWS
## Key Features & Visual Previews
-	Page 1 (Overview): Total Sales ($2.26M), Orders (4.9K), YoY Growth & Trend line chart. 
-	Page 2 (Product): Top 5 best-products, Sub-category contribution %, Ship mode breakdown. 
-	Page 3 (Customer): Segment breakdown, Top 10 high-value customers & regional flow. 
## Key Business Insights & Recommendations section:
-	Q4 Peak Trend: Sales surge consistently every Q4; build up inventory in advance to prepare for peak demand. 
-	Revenue Drivers: Technology & Office Supplies (specifically Phones and Chairs) generate the highest sales contribution. 
-	Customer Retention: Implement targeted promotional programs and incentives for top 10 VIP customers to drive retention. 
## How to Run
-	Download and open `superstore.pbix` in Power BI Desktop.
