## Coffee Shop Sales & Operations Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Objective
Analyze 149K+ sales transactions (Jan–Jun 2023) across 3 New York store locations using Microsoft Excel to track revenue trends, peak operating hours, and top-performing categories for operational optimization.

## Tools & Concepts Used
-	Tool: Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables,Advanced Formulas, Interactive Charts, Slicers)
-	Concepts:Data Cleaning & Structuring, Pivot Data Modeling, Time Slot Aggregation, Dynamic Dashboard Layout & UI Design

## Key Features & Visual Previews
- KPI Scorecards: Automated summary cards for Total Revenue ($698.8K), Transactions (149.1K), and Units Sold (214.4K).
- Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filtering by Store Location, Month, and Category.
- Peak Hour Chart: Hourly sales distribution highlighting Morning Rush demand.
- Category Breakdown: Revenue contribution across 9 product categories.

## Key Business Insights & Recommendations
- Core Drivers:Coffee ($270K / 38.6%) and Tea ($196K / 28.1%) generate over 66% of total revenue.Recommendation: Bundle high-margin Bakery items with beverage orders during morning peak hours.
- Location Balance: Hell's Kitchen led total revenue ($236.5K), while Lower Manhattan recorded the highest average basket size ($4.81).Recommendation:Optimize inventory & staffing at Lower Manhattan during morning rush.

## How to Run / Files
- Download or clone `Coffee Shop Sales.xlsx`.
- Open in Microsoft Excel (2016 or newer recommended).
- Navigate across tabs: Data (Raw Data), Pivot (Pivot Tables), and Dashboard (Interactive View).
